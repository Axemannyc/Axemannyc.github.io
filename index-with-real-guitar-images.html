01 — The Collection

Four legendary instruments.

1964

Fender Stratocaster

The definitive electric guitar design. Its sculpted body, three-pickup layout and unmistakable tone became a blueprint for generations.

1959

Gibson Les Paul Standard

A carved maple top, mahogany construction and powerful humbuckers created one of rock's defining sounds.

1965 / MODERN ERA

Fender Johnny Marr Jaguar

A distinctive Jaguar configuration associated with one of Britain's most influential guitarists and the sound of alternative guitar music.

1962

Gibson ES-335

The semi-hollow classic that bridged the gap between solid-body power and warm hollow-body resonance.

02 — A Brief History

More than instruments.

The greatest guitars are more than combinations of wood, metal and electronics. They become part of the language of music itself — recognised instantly by their shape, their sound and the players who made them famous.

1950sThe solid-body electric guitar changes popular music.
1960sBritish rock and American blues turn electric guitars into cultural icons.
1970sRock becomes louder, heavier and more technically ambitious.
1980s → TodayThe classic designs continue to influence almost every generation of guitarists.
